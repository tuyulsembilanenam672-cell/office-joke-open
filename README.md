Entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: The Office Joke Open** on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232):
one line of office comedy, at most 200 characters, judged head to head
by an AI judge. Free entry, $2 to the single funniest joke.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing is built or
run beyond that; the writing is the whole contest.

## The joke

> I hit reply-all to ask everyone to stop replying all, and 47 people replied all to agree.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
